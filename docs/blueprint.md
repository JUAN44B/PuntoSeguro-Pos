# **App Name**: PuntoSeguro POS

## Core Features:

- Product Management: Comprehensive CRUD operations for products including code, name, description, category, stock, purchase price, sales price, tax, discounts, profit margin, and supplier information.
- Offline Sales Transactions: Record sales transactions with date, time, employee, customer, products, quantities, and prices; supports cash, card, transfer, and voucher payments; offers quick sales without customer registration.
- Automated Stock Alerts: Implement automatic alerts for low stock levels to prevent shortages.
- Customer Relationship Management: Register and classify customers, track purchase history, and manage credit accounts.
- Inventory Control: Manage stock adjustments with reasons (shrinkage, return, manual adjustment), and provide reports for low stock, dead stock, and best-selling products.
- Sales Reports Generation: Tool to create detailed reports for sales by day, week, month, year, product, category, customer and employee. LLM intelligently includes all details from a knowledge base.
- Role-Based User Management: Secure user login with roles and permissions (administrator, cashier, supervisor, inventory manager), plus activity log.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep blue (#3F51B5) to convey security and robustness.
- Background color: Light gray (#F5F5F5), close to white to ensure high readability, for the interface background.
- Accent color: Vibrant orange (#FF9800) to highlight key actions and elements, such as call-to-action buttons.
- Font: 'PT Sans', a humanist sans-serif, for both headings and body text, to provide a balance of modernity and approachability.
- Use clean, simple icons from Material Design to maintain a consistent and modern interface.
- Employ a modular layout, leveraging Material Design's grid system to ensure scalability and responsiveness.
- Implement subtle animations for transitions and feedback, enhancing the user experience without being distracting.
